# Yala v1.0.0 — Release Notes

**Release:** v1.0.0  
**Date:** 2026-07-21  
**Market:** Nouakchott, Mauritania  
**Platforms:** Android (Rider, Driver, Delivery) · Web Admin  

---

## Welcome to Yala v1.0

Yala v1.0 is the first commercial release of Mauritania's integrated mobility platform — ride-hailing, courier delivery, and driver earnings in one ecosystem.

---

## What's included

### For riders
- Request rides (Regular, XL, Comfort, Share)
- Live driver tracking and in-app chat
- Secure PIN verification at pickup
- Wallet payments and ride history
- Saved places and referral rewards

### For drivers
- Go online/offline with document compliance checks
- Smart ride offers with acceptance scoring
- Navigation, arrive, start, and complete flows
- Real-time earnings and performance dashboard
- Wallet and cash-out to Bankily, Masravi, Sedad, or bank account

### For couriers (Delivery)
- Multi-category deliveries (food, pharmacy, grocery, parcels)
- PIN-verified pickup and dropoff
- Proof-of-delivery photos
- 80% courier earnings · transparent pricing

### For operations (Admin)
- Executive Dashboard and Operations Center
- AI Operations (dispatch insights, surge, hotspots)
- Business Operations Hub (CRM, marketing, corporate)
- Fleet Performance Center
- Finance Operations & Reconciliation
- Multi-City Operations Platform
- Smart Pricing & Dispatch Engine
- Launch Command Center and Growth Dashboard

---

## Improvements since RC2

- **Performance:** Faster admin dashboards (query optimization, caching)
- **Reliability:** Improved health checks and Celery monitoring
- **Mobile stability:** Rider cancel cleanup, driver online UX, ride state sync (RC3)
- **Operations:** Finance reconciliation, multi-city admin, smart pricing simulator

## Known limitations (v1.0.0)

- Nouakchott pilot market (multi-city admin ready; expansion ops-controlled)
- Android only — iOS coming in a future release
- Smart Pricing Engine disabled by default (legacy pricing active)
- Closed beta capacity caps may apply during initial rollout
