## Full Description (4000 chars max)

Yala Delivery connects couriers with customers for fast, secure package delivery across Nouakchott and Mauritania.

Whether it's food from your favorite restaurant, medicine from the pharmacy, groceries, documents, or parcels — Yala Delivery gets it there safely with real-time tracking and PIN-verified handoff.

**For Couriers:**
• Accept delivery requests instantly
• Navigate to pickup and dropoff with one tap
• Verify pickup with 4-digit PIN from sender
• Complete delivery with recipient PIN + proof photo
• Track your earnings in real time
• Flexible schedule — go online when you want

**Delivery Categories:**
• 🍔 Food — Restaurant meals delivered hot
• 💊 Pharmacy — Medicines with careful handling
• 🛒 Grocery — Supermarket and market items
• 📦 Parcel — Boxes and packages of any size
• 📄 Documents — Envelopes and secure papers
• 🛍️ Shopping — Retail pickups

**Security & Trust:**
• PIN verification at pickup and dropoff
• Proof-of-delivery photo required
• Masked calls between courier and customer
• Real-time GPS tracking for every delivery
• In-app chat for quick coordination

**Earnings:**
• 80% of every fare goes to the courier
• Transparent pricing — see earnings before accepting
• Daily earnings dashboard
• No minimum hours — work on your schedule

**How it works:**
1. Go online on the courier dashboard
2. Accept a delivery request
3. Navigate to pickup location
4. Confirm pickup with sender's PIN
5. Deliver to recipient
6. Complete with recipient PIN + photo proof
7. Earnings added to your wallet

Built for Mauritania. Prices in MRU. Service across Nouakchott wilayas.

Download Yala Delivery and start earning today.
