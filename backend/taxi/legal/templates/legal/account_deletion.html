Account Deletion

This page is published by Yala Technologies for users of Yala Rider, Yala Driver, and Yala Delivery. You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data by contacting our support team.

How to request deletion

Email support@yalataxi.live with the subject line "Account deletion request". Include the email address or phone number used in the Yala Rider, Yala Driver, or Yala Delivery app so we can verify the account.

There is no self-serve deletion API. Requests are handled by support after identity verification.

What information to provide

  • The email address or phone number on the account
  • Which app the account uses: Yala Rider, Yala Driver, or Yala Delivery
  • Any other detail that helps us verify you own the account

Data deleted

After verification, we delete or anonymize account profile information, name, email address, phone number, personal account details, and other personal data associated with the account when deletion is allowed. Driver documents are deleted within 30 days of account closure.

Data that may be retained

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Ride history and payment records are kept for 3 years for accounting and dispute resolution. After account deletion, we may retain anonymized data for analytics.

Some ride records, transaction records, cancellation records, safety records, fraud prevention records, or legal records may be retained when required for security, dispute resolution, legal compliance, financial reporting, or operational safety. We keep personal data only as long as needed to provide the app, maintain records, comply with legal obligations, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, and support safety or transaction requirements.

Processing time

Deletion requests are processed after identity verification. We will respond to the request using the contact information provided by the user.

Privacy questions without deleting your account

Users may contact support@yalataxi.live for privacy questions or data requests. Some data may be required to keep the account active and provide taxi booking and delivery services. See the Yala Privacy Policy.
