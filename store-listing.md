## Yala Driver

**App Name:** Yala Driver
**Short Description (80 chars):**
Drive with Yala in Mauritania. Earn money on your schedule.

**Full Description:**
Yala Driver lets you earn money by giving rides in Mauritania. Go online when you want, accept rides nearby, and get paid.

Features:
• Go online/offline anytime — drive on your schedule
• Receive ride requests with sound alerts and vibration
• See rider pickup location, destination, and fare before accepting
• Navigation to pickup and drop-off (Google Maps or Waze)
• Slide controls: Arrive → Start Ride → Finish Ride
• PIN verification for rider identity at pickup
• Track your earnings daily, weekly, and monthly
• Driver Level system: Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Elite
• Upload and manage documents (License, Insurance, Carte Grise, Vignette)
• Hall of Fame recognition for top drivers
• SOS emergency safety button
• Multi-language support (French, Arabic, English)

Earn More:
• Higher levels unlock bonus multipliers (up to 1.5x)
• Priority ride matching for top-rated drivers
• Tips from satisfied riders
• Peak hour bonuses

Requirements:
• Valid driver's license
• Vehicle insurance
• Vehicle registration (Carte Grise)
• Annual inspection (Vignette)
• Smartphone with GPS

Available in Nouakchott, Mauritania. More cities coming soon.

**Category:** Maps & Navigation
**Content Rating:** Everyone
**Privacy Policy URL:** https://www.yalataxi.live/privacy
