## 2. Full description

**English:**

Yala Driver is the partner app for professional drivers and couriers in Mauritania. **Earn more**, **drive smart**, and **deliver safely** — with every tool you need to go online, accept work, and track your performance.

**Earn more**
• Transparent earnings dashboard — today, this week, and monthly totals
• See fare, distance, and payout before you accept
• Level up from Bronze to Elite for better priority and bonuses
• Accept rides and delivery requests from one account

**Drive smart**
• Go online or offline on your schedule
• Clear trip workflow: accept → arrive → verify PIN → start → complete
• Sound alerts and vibration for new ride requests
• Navigation to pickup and drop-off
• In-app chat and call without sharing your personal number

**Deliver safely**
• Enable delivery mode for package and food requests
• Pickup and drop-off PIN verification
• Proof-of-delivery support for couriers
• SOS safety tools on every trip

**Documents & compliance**
• Upload license, insurance, carte grise, vignette, and ID in-app
• Track approval status and expiry reminders
• Stay approved and ready to go online

**Analytics dashboard**
• Acceptance rate, rating, completed rides, and earnings trends
• Performance metrics to help you improve and earn more
• Achievement badges and driver level progress

**Built for Mauritania**
• French, Arabic, and English
• Nouakchott and expanding nationwide coverage
• Local driver support

Join Yala Driver — **Earn more • Drive smart • Deliver safely**.
