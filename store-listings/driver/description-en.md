# Yala Driver - Earn Money

Drive with Yala and earn money on your schedule in Mauritania. Accept ride requests, navigate to passengers, and track your earnings in real-time.

## Features

• Accept rides instantly
• Real-time navigation
• Earnings dashboard
• Driver level system (Bronze to Elite)
• Share ride support (earn more per trip)
• Document management
• Achievement badges
• Available in French, English, and Arabic
