Yala Taxi account support

Account Deletion

Yala Taxi users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data by contacting our support team.

How to request deletion

Email support@yalataxi.live with the subject line "Account deletion request". Include the email address or phone number used in the Yala Taxi app so we can verify the account.

Data Deleted

After verification, we delete or anonymize account profile information, name, email address, phone number, personal account details, and other personal data associated with the account when deletion is allowed.

Data That May Be Retained

Some ride records, transaction records, cancellation records, safety records, fraud prevention records, or legal records may be retained when required for security, dispute resolution, legal compliance, financial reporting, or operational safety.

Processing Time

Deletion requests are processed after identity verification. We will respond to the request using the contact information provided by the user.

Delete Data Without Deleting Account

Users may contact support@yalataxi.live for privacy questions or data requests. Some data may be required to keep the account active and provide taxi booking services.
