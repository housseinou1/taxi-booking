Privacy Policy

Yala Technologies provides ride-booking and delivery services in Mauritania through three applications: Yala Rider (for riders), Yala Driver (for taxi drivers) and Yala Delivery (for delivery couriers and delivery customers). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how users can contact us about privacy or account requests.

Information We Collect

We may collect account and contact information such as name, email address, phone number, username, password authentication data, and user profile details. We may also collect location information, pickup and destination details, ride history, rider spending history, driver earnings history, cancellation records, app activity, device details, diagnostics, and support messages.

Payment information is processed by our payment providers. Depending on the method selected, transaction details may be shared with Bankily, Masravi, Seddad, a card processor, or a bank in order to complete the payment. Yala does not store full payment card numbers: card details saved in the app are limited to the card holder name, card type, expiry date and the last four digits.

How We Use Information

We use information to create and manage accounts, provide taxi booking features, match riders with drivers, show pickup and destination locations, track active rides, manage completed and cancelled rides, calculate spending and earnings, provide admin analytics, improve app reliability, prevent fraud, support user safety, and respond to support requests.

Location Data

Location data is used for pickup selection, destination selection, driver assignment, ride tracking, and trip management. Location information is used only as needed to provide taxi booking and ride management features.

Background Location (Yala Driver and Yala Delivery)

Yala Driver and Yala Delivery share the driver's or courier's location with Yala while a trip or delivery is active, including when the app is minimised or the device screen is locked. This lets riders and delivery customers follow the trip or delivery in real time and lets Yala operate dispatch, tracking and safety features.

On Android this uses a location foreground service. While it is running the app shows a permanent notification (for example, "Yala Driver is tracking your location"), and it stops as soon as the trip or delivery is completed or cancelled, or when the user signs out. Yala Rider does not request background location. Background location is never used for advertising or for any purpose unrelated to an active trip or delivery.

Data Sharing

Yala may share limited ride information between riders and drivers only when needed to complete a ride. This may include rider name, driver details, pickup location, destination, ride status, and contact information. Administrators may access operational data needed to manage users, rides, cancellations, revenue, support, and safety.

We may also use service providers for hosting, maps, notifications, analytics, diagnostics, support, or payment processing. These providers process information only as needed to provide their services.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user data. User data collected by the app is encrypted during transfer when sent over HTTPS or other secure service connections.

Data Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed to provide the app, maintain records, comply with legal obligations, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, and support safety or transaction requirements.

After an account is closed, driver and courier documents are deleted within 30 days. Ride history and payment records are kept for 3 years for accounting, dispute resolution and legal obligations. Some ride, transaction, cancellation, safety, fraud-prevention or legal records may be retained where required for security, dispute resolution, legal compliance, financial reporting or operational safety. Anonymised data may be retained for analytics.

Account Deletion

Users can request account deletion by visiting yalataxi.live/account-deletion or by emailing support@yalataxi.live.

Contact

For privacy questions, support, or account requests, contact support@yalataxi.live.
