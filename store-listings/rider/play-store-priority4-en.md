## 2. Full description

**English:**

Yala Rider is Mauritania's mobility app — built to get you where you need to go **fast**, keep every trip **safe**, and deliver a **reliable** experience from booking to drop-off.

Whether you're heading across Nouakchott or planning a trip beyond the capital, Yala Rider puts a verified driver on your map in seconds.

**Why riders choose Yala**
• **Fast** — Book in seconds with GPS pickup, clear fare estimates, and quick driver matching
• **Safe** — Pickup PIN verification, live trip sharing, SOS support, and verified driver profiles
• **Reliable** — Real-time tracking, transparent pricing, and trip history with receipts

**Book your way**
• Regular, Comfort, XL, and Share ride options
• See estimated fare before you confirm
• Schedule rides in advance, including airport pickup and drop-off
• Pay with cash, card, or Yala Wallet

**Stay in control**
• Track your driver live on the map
• Share your trip with family or friends
• Rate drivers and view your ride history
• Manage referrals, Lost & Found, and account settings in one app

**Delivery from Yala Rider**
• Request package and parcel delivery without switching apps
• Track your courier from pickup to drop-off

**Made for Mauritania**
• French, Arabic, and English support
• Built for Nouakchott and expanding across Mauritania
• Local support when you need help

Download Yala Rider today — **Fast • Safe • Reliable** mobility, wherever you're going.
