# Yala Rider - Book a Ride

Book affordable rides in Mauritania with Yala. Choose from Regular, Share, Comfort, or XL rides. Track your driver in real-time, pay securely, and rate your experience.

## Features

• Book rides instantly
• Share rides to save up to 50%
• Real-time driver tracking
• Secure in-app payments
• Rate and review drivers
• Ride history and receipts
• Emergency support button
• Available in French, English, and Arabic
