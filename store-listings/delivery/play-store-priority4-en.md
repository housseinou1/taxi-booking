## 2. Full description

**English:**

Yala Delivery is the courier app for Yala's delivery network in Mauritania. **Fast delivery**, **live tracking**, and **secure handoff** — built for professionals who pick up, deliver, and earn with confidence.

**Courier dashboard**
• Go online or offline on your schedule
• See today's earnings, completed deliveries, and performance at a glance
• Analytics snapshot: acceptance rate, rating, and daily totals
• Clear status for every active delivery

**Parcel delivery**
• Accept package, document, food, and essential delivery requests
• See pickup, drop-off, distance, and payout before you accept
• Sound alerts for new delivery offers
• Complete workflow from accept to delivery confirmation

**Live tracking**
• Navigate pickup and drop-off with map guidance
• Customers and senders follow progress in real time
• Status updates from assigned → picked up → in transit → delivered

**PIN verification**
• Verify pickup with secure PIN at collection
• Confirm drop-off with recipient PIN before completing delivery
• Reduce disputes and protect every handoff

**Proof of delivery**
• Photo confirmation at the door when required
• Instant completion updates for sender and receiver
• Reliable delivery records for support and earnings

**Built for Mauritania**
• French, Arabic, and English
• Designed for Nouakchott and expanding courier coverage
• Local support for couriers on the Yala platform

Download Yala Delivery — **Fast delivery • Live tracking • Secure handoff**.
