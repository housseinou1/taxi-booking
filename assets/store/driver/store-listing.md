## Full Description (4000 chars max)

Yala Driver is the professional partner app for drivers on the Yala platform. Earn money on your schedule, track your performance, and level up your career.

💰 EARN 80% OF EVERY FARE
Keep most of what you earn. Transparent earnings breakdown for every ride. Cash out your earnings anytime.

📊 REAL-TIME EARNINGS DASHBOARD
See today's earnings, weekly totals, and monthly performance at a glance. Bar charts show your earning trends over time.

🏆 LEVEL SYSTEM — BRONZE TO ELITE
• Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Elite
• Higher levels unlock better ride priority and bonuses
• Track your progress with clear metrics: acceptance rate, rating, ride count

🚗 CLEAR WORKFLOW
1. Go Online
2. Accept ride requests
3. Navigate to pickup
4. Verify rider PIN
5. Start trip
6. Complete ride

No confusion. Every step is clear.

📦 DELIVERY MODE
Switch on Delivery Mode to accept package deliveries alongside rides. Food, documents, pharmacy, shopping — earn from every type of delivery.

🚌 INTERCITY TRIPS
Accept long-distance trips between cities. Higher fares, longer routes. Nouakchott ↔ Nouadhibou, Atar, Rosso, and more.

📄 DOCUMENTS CENTER
Upload all required documents (National ID, License, Insurance, Vignette, Carte Grise) directly in the app. Track expiry dates and approval status.

💬 IN-APP COMMUNICATION
Chat with riders without sharing your personal number. Call button for quick pickup coordination.

📈 PERFORMANCE METRICS
• Total rides completed
• Acceptance rate
• Average rating
• Completion rate
• Reward points earned

🎯 ACHIEVEMENTS & REWARDS
Earn badges for milestones: first ride, 100 rides, 5-star streaks, and more. Accumulate reward points for bonus payouts.

🛡️ DRIVER SAFETY
• Emergency SOS button on every ride
• Incident reporting
• GPS location sharing with Yala support
• Rider ratings help you avoid problem riders

⚙️ SETTINGS
• Language: English, Français, العربية
• Dark mode
• GPS accuracy control
• Notification preferences
• Privacy controls

REQUIREMENTS
• Valid Mauritania driving license
• Vehicle registration (Carte Grise)
• Insurance document
• Vignette
• National ID
• Admin approval before going online

ABOUT YALA
Yala connects drivers with riders across Mauritania. We're building the country's leading mobility platform with fair earnings, professional tools, and nationwide coverage.

Join thousands of Yala drivers earning every day! 🇲🇷
