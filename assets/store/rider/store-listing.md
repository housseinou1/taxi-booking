## Full Description (4000 chars max)

Yala Rider is Mauritania's leading ride-hailing app. Book a ride, track your driver in real-time, and arrive safely — all from your phone.

🚗 BOOK A RIDE IN SECONDS
Enter your pickup and destination, choose your ride type (Regular, XL, or Comfort), and confirm. A verified driver will be on the way within minutes.

📍 REAL-TIME TRACKING
Watch your driver approach on a live map. See the ETA update as they get closer. Know exactly when they'll arrive.

🛡️ YOUR SAFETY COMES FIRST
• Ride PIN — Your driver must enter your unique PIN before starting the trip
• Share Trip — Let family and friends follow your ride live
• SOS Button — One tap for emergency help
• Verified Drivers — Every driver passes ID, license, and insurance checks

🏙️ ALL OF MAURITANIA
Available in Nouakchott, Nouadhibou, Rosso, Kaédi, Kiffa, Atar, and 70+ more cities across all 15 wilayas.

🚌 INTERCITY TRAVEL
Book long-distance trips between cities. Fixed pricing, no surprises. Nouakchott to Nouadhibou, Rosso, Atar, and more.

📦 DELIVERY SERVICE
Send packages, documents, food, pharmacy items, and shopping — with the same trusted Yala drivers. Track every delivery live.

⏰ SCHEDULE RIDES
Book rides in advance for airport pickups, meetings, or any planned trip.

💬 IN-APP CHAT
Message your driver directly within the app. No need to share your phone number.

💰 TRANSPARENT PRICING
See the fare before you book. No hidden charges. All prices in MRU.

🎁 PROMO CODES
Enter promo codes for discounts. Refer friends and earn credits.

ABOUT YALA
Yala is Mauritania's mobility platform. We connect riders with verified, professional drivers across the country. Our mission: make transportation safe, affordable, and accessible for every Mauritanian.

Download Yala Rider today and ride anywhere! 🇲🇷
